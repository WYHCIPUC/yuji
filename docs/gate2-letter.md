**Subject:** Permission inquiry — educational use of a CHGIS V6 subset on a free public website

Dear CHGIS Team,

I am writing on behalf of the "Yuji" (禹迹) project — a free, non-commercial, publicly accessible educational website that presents the historical geography of China as an interactive narrative (Chinese-language, no advertising, no fees, no sponsored content).

We would like to use a small derived subset of CHGIS V6, and we are seeking your written confirmation that this use is consistent with the CHGIS V6 EULA:

1. **Data used (subset only):** a filtered portion of the V6 Time Series county seat points and prefecture seat points (presence years filtered to ≤ 1602 CE), plus simplified polygons derived from the V6 Time Series prefecture polygons. After filtering, transformation, and deduplication, the derived dataset is roughly a few thousand point records.
2. **Transformation, not redistribution:** the original shapefiles are never redistributed or offered for download. Only a simplified, re-formatted JSON derived from the subset is embedded in the site; the prefecture polygons are clearly labeled on-screen as "mathematically allocated (Voronoi) schematic zones, not actual historical boundaries".
3. **Attribution:** the site credits "CHGIS, Version 6" (c) Fairbank Center for Chinese Studies and the Institute for Chinese Historical Geography at Fudan University, with a link to the source datasets and a Chinese-language description of exactly how the data was filtered and transformed.
4. **Non-commercial use:** the site is free and educational. We acknowledge that any future commercialization would require re-verification and possibly a commercial license.

The Dataverse metadata for these datasets is marked "CC0 1.0", while the terms-of-use text points to the CHGIS V6 EULA (free for academic research, no commercial use, resale, or redistribution). To be cautious we follow the stricter EULA reading, and we would greatly appreciate your confirmation that the use described above is acceptable.

If any part of this use raises concerns, we will gladly adjust or remove the material.

Thank you for your work on this remarkable dataset, and thank you in advance for your time.

Respectfully,
